Scope, Revision Limits & Handoff

Transparency is key at Batonic. Know exactly what's included before you commit.

01

Revision Limits & Workflow

To maintain efficiency and guarantee fast delivery, the Batonic Business preview includes two structured review rounds:

  • Round 1: Visuals & Branding Review color palette, typography, layout structure, and overall aesthetic.
  • Round 2: Copy & Final Polish Final adjustments to textual content, links, contact form endpoints, and responsive check.
Additional custom revisions beyond the 2 standard rounds are billed hourly or via custom add-on.
02

Launch Handoff Options

Once you approve the final preview, we ensure a frictionless handover so your site goes live immediately without technical friction:

  • Clean Source Code Handover Fully commented HTML, Tailwind CSS, and JS files delivered directly to your GitHub or zip.
  • Direct Server Deployment We deploy the site directly to your domain and hosting with a walkthrough guide.
Includes a 30-day bug warranty and free documentation.
